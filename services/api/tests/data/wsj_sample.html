Apple Reports Strong Q2 Earnings

Apple Inc. reported quarterly earnings that exceeded analysts' expectations, driven by strong iPhone sales in China and continued growth in services revenue.

The company posted earnings per share of $1.52, beating the consensus estimate of $1.43. Revenue rose 8% year over year to $94.8 billion.

"We are pleased with our performance this quarter," said CEO Tim Cook.
